B2B in the panel

Screens for selling to businesses: companies and their buyers, credit and terms, quotes, approvals, receivables, dealer territories and the leads routed to them. Since the first version: purchase-order search and marking several orders paid at once, a business-order card on the order screen, states read by code or name everywhere (D72), and a console for running many stores from one platform (D73).

What changed in the engine

Each change came from something the screens could not do well. Every one is covered by tests, in the OpenAPI contract, and described in the skills.

ProblemBeforeNow
State names A state was matched as text, so "California" never reached the dealer for "CA". The seeded enquiry from Cupertino went to the nationwide dealer. States in the US, Canada, Australia and India are read by code or name on both sides: "California", "ca" and "US-CA" are all CA, and territories are stored as codes. India's codes changed in 2023 (OR → OD) and both still work. The Cupertino enquiry now reaches Golden State Dealers. Decision D72 (1852310) moved the rule into core, so tax rates and shipping zones read states the same way: a rate typed as "California" is stored as CA, and rules saved under a name earlier still match without being rewritten.
One lead No route read a single lead, so a lead could not be linked to. GET /api/admin/x/b2b/leads/{id} under leads.read. The panel opens /b2b/leads?lead=5 straight onto that lead.
Product names A lead carried product and variant ids only, so the drawer said "Product 1". Every lead names its product_title and variant_sku as the catalogue has them now; a deleted product leaves the ids.
Finding leads No search, and no way to tell leads the store handed over from ones a territory matched. ?q= searches name, message and postcode, and email and phone except on a demo store, where they are masked. ?routed_by= filters by how a lead arrived. Pairs that cannot overlap are refused with a 400 that names them.
Picking a dealer Pickers showed the first 200 companies, dealers or not. Companies carry territory_count, and ?dealers=true keeps those with a territory. Pickers list dealers first and still offer every dealer past 200 companies.
Finding a PO A company quotes its PO number when it pays, and the ledger could only be narrowed to overdue orders. ?q= on receivables, a company's orders and the buyer's GET /x/b2b/orders, matched against PO number, order number and, except on a demo store, the buyer's email (c960ee0).
One business order The order screen showed the module's metadata as a raw line. GET /api/admin/x/b2b/orders/{order_id} reads one order's ledger row: company, PO, who placed it, due date, overdue, and the quote or approval it came from. The order screen's Business order card is built on it.
Paying several Each invoice had to be opened and marked paid on its own. Receivables marks the ticked orders paid through core's mark-paid, one order at a time, so each keeps its own event and history. No new route.
Many stores Multi-tenant mode (D70) was an API with no screens: a platform operator had curl. The platform's hosts serve the panel's /platform screens through core's ServeAdminPanel, the same files, cache rules and CSP a store sends; every other path there is still a JSON 404. The health report gained base_domain. Decision D73, e659cee.
Verification

How it was checked

In a real browser with Playwright, against the built binary on fresh databases: a dev store, and a platform on its own port serving stores at <slug>.localhost. The browser checks ran at least twice each.

  • PASSAll 20 screen URLs at 1440 px light, 1440 px dark and 390 px: no console or page errors, no failed requests, no sideways scrolling. Twice, then once more with reduced motion.
  • PASSCompanies and quotes: create a company, edit its terms, add a buyer who joins at once and one who is invited, price a quote line by line, send it, change it and send it again, decline one.
  • PASSTerritories and leads: add a territory, see the conflict naming the other dealer, take it back, filter the Territories tab, search leads, open one from a link, hand it to a dealer, see a closed dealer refused, change its status, take it back.
  • PASSKeyboard: list rows open with Enter, a lead opens from its row the same way, and the overdue tile shows a focus ring.
  • PASSBusiness orders: the card names the company, PO, on account and Overdue; searching "po-4490" finds that one order in Receivables and on the company's page; ticking two unpaid orders offers "Mark paid (2) · $1,550.00" and both are marked paid. The company page's search box and filter are one height.
  • PASSPlatform console, at desktop, dark and 390 px: the platform's root goes to the console and shows no store login; a wrong token is refused in words; a new store opens with its slug following the name, and its owner signs in to its own panel at its own host with the generated password; rename, attach and detach a domain, add an owner, replace the token (the old one answers 401, the new one 200), suspend (its host answers 503), resume, and delete only with the slug typed exactly; signing out forgets the token.
  • PASSGo, through c960ee0: the full suite (46 packages), the API-only build and its core tests, gofmt, go vet, the docs check, the 59-step smoke sale and gocommerce doctor. For the console: the platform package with and without the panel, core's panel tests, gofmt, go vet, the API-only build, the docs check and the smoke sale.
  • PASSMotion: pages slide 3 px and fade in over 150 ms, drawers over 200 ms. Frames held a 16.7 ms median with none over 25 ms. With reduced motion, slides become plain fades, on the console too. A refused platform token shakes once (240 ms) and a copied credential's icon pops (200 ms), both transform only.
Not done

Still open

  • OPENThe platform console (e659cee) is committed on b2b-territories-leads but not pushed to main. Everything before it, through c960ee0, is on main.
  • OPENStatements and a credit-limit history were asked about but not defined: what a statement covers and whether a limit change is audited are decisions to make before building either.
  • OPENThe platform lists every store at once. Past a few thousand stores the list wants paging from the engine; until then the console filters what it has.
  • OPENApprovals carry no company name, Receivables cannot show "owed but not yet due", and a quote's expiry cannot be cleared once set.
