# 360° Marketing Engine — Content & Positioning Brief

## Objective

Expand the commerce platform story beyond storefront, catalog, checkout, and order management.

The new positioning should communicate that the platform can become a **360° marketing engine** that helps a business:

- acquire customers
- understand customer behavior
- convert more visitors
- recover lost revenue
- retain customers
- increase repeat purchases
- automate campaigns
- personalize communication
- measure performance across channels
- connect marketing activity directly with commerce outcomes

The core message should be:

> **Commerce should not stop at checkout.**
>
> The same system that powers products, customers, carts, orders, and payments should also help acquire, convert, retain, and reactivate customers.

## Commerce + Marketing in One Engine

# 8. Customer Segmentation

## Heading

**Build segments from real commerce behavior.**

Examples:

### High-value customers

Customers with lifetime value above a threshold.

### Frequent buyers

Customers who purchased multiple times recently.

### At-risk customers

Customers whose purchase frequency is declining.

### Cart abandoners

Customers who added products but did not complete checkout.

### Product-interest segments

Customers repeatedly viewing a category or product type.

### Discount-sensitive customers

Customers who mainly purchase using offers.

### VIP customers

Top spenders or highest-margin customers.

### New customers

First purchase within a recent period.

### Dormant customers

No purchase for 60/90/180 days.

Allow dynamic segments that update automatically.

# 50. Implementation Rules

1. Marketing must use real commerce events.
2. Do not treat email as the entire marketing system.
3. WhatsApp should be first-class for Indian merchants.
4. All channels must share one customer profile.
5. Build dynamic segments instead of static lists only.
6. Every automation must have a measurable business outcome.
7. Tie campaigns to orders and revenue.
8. Respect channel consent and unsubscribe preferences.
9. Add frequency controls.
10. Make marketing modules optional and extensible.
11. Use actual commerce data for AI recommendations.
12. Do not claim autonomous AI features until they exist.
13. Label future capabilities as roadmap items.
14. Show real dashboards when implemented.
15. Prefer measurable proof over broad AI claims.
16. Design for both D2C and B2B use cases.
17. Support event-driven workflows.
18. Keep the system developer-friendly and API-first.
19. Allow external providers to plug into email, SMS, WhatsApp, and ads.
20. Keep ownership of customer and campaign data with the merchant.
